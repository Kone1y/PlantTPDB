For the answerable benchmark questions, generated answers were evaluated independently for factual accuracy, completeness, and evidence coherence using a prespecified 0–100 anchored scoring rubric. For each dimension, scores of 0, 25, 50, 75, and 100 represented increasing levels of performance, with intermediate scores permitted when an answer fell between adjacent anchors.

For factual accuracy, 100 indicated that all or nearly all substantive factual content was consistent with the reference standard, without meaningful factual errors or misleading interpretations; 75 indicated that the answer correctly conveyed the central scientific conclusions and most major factual information, although some minor inaccuracies, imprecisions, or oversimplifications could be present without materially affecting the overall interpretation; 50 indicated that the answer contained a meaningful amount of correct information but also notable factual errors or uncertain statements, such that the response was only moderately reliable overall; 25 indicated that major factual errors affected much of the answer or its central conclusion; and 0 indicated that the answer was fundamentally incorrect or did not provide a factually valid response.

For completeness, 100 indicated that all or nearly all essential elements of the reference answer were covered, including the principal mechanisms, key findings, and relevant distinctions, with no important substantive omissions; 75 indicated that the answer adequately addressed the central question and covered the principal mechanisms, findings, or comparisons, although some supporting information, species-specific examples, experimental details, or secondary findings could be omitted without substantially compromising the overall answer; 50 indicated that the answer contained meaningful relevant information and addressed part of the required content, but several major elements could be missing; 25 indicated only limited coverage of the required information, with most central elements absent; and 0 indicated that the essential information was absent or that the response did not meaningfully address the question. Essential elements were operationalized as the R-level gold facts of the question, with greater emphasis placed on facts directly relevant to the central question.

For evidence coherence, 100 indicated that the evidence supplied to or cited by the system was directly relevant to and consistently supported all or nearly all substantive factual claims, without meaningful discrepancies; 75 indicated that the evidence was generally relevant and consistent with the central conclusions and major factual claims, although some supporting statements could rely on broader or indirect evidence, or lack the most specific available citation, without materially weakening the overall evidentiary basis; 50 indicated that the evidence was partly relevant and supported some of the answer, but several claims had weak, incomplete, or unclear correspondence to the accompanying evidence; 25 indicated substantial mismatch between the answer and the accompanying evidence; and 0 indicated that the supplied evidence did not support, or materially contradicted, the central factual content of the answer.
